1. What Is a Matrix?

A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers arranged in rows and columns. Matrices are the core structure of linear algebra and are ubiquitous in mathematics and its applied fields.

Typical applications of matrices:

  • Data Science: data tables, feature matrices, machine learning model parameters
  • Computer Graphics: rotation, scaling, transformation
  • Engineering and Physics: solving linear equations, system modeling
  • Financial Mathematics: risk analysis, linear models
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI/ML): neural network weights, vector operations

In short, a matrix is a powerful structured data representation form and the cornerstone of modern mathematical systems.
